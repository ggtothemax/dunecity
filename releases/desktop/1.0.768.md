# Dune City 1.0.768

AI economies grow their refinery and harvester capacity earlier while continuing city development. Core production buildings take priority over large turret networks. Police target dangerous crime, rocket turrets protect valuable buildings and expansions, and ornithopters respond to attacks on bases and harvesters. Policing budgets are limited to 33% early and 50% with healthy finances; funding increases are smoothed. New expansions aim for three rocket turrets before the next expansion proceeds. Emergency Starport MCV recovery works on every difficulty.

The metaserver now provides maps and mods directly in the game and editors, replacing Workshop navigation. Map selection supports mod, size and player filters. Maps carry version metadata, are classified as Vanilla, Tornie or DuneCity from their content, and can be converted in the editor. Save As creates a separate map identity. The online catalogue includes the seeded local collection.

Earned credits share refinery and silo storage capacity, and total holdings cannot exceed 999999. Unspent starting credits retain their allowance; construction refunds preserve the starting-credit portion actually paid. Match statistics show tax collected with the original yellow styling.

Multiplayer protocol 24: all participants must update together. Save format 9846 records refund funding sources; older saves remain loadable, with legacy queued-order refunds conservatively limited by storage capacity.

Validated with 31 native regression targets, 221 signaling-service integration tests, and bounded Medium/Hard AI matches. Balance checks reduced construction-yard losses and funding churn on the tested map; they do not guarantee that expansions survive every attack.
