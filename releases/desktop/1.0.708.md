# Dune City 1.0.708

DuneCity can now use selectable SimCity or Dune2 graphics skins without changing
simulation rules or the 2x2 city-zone footprint. Campaign selection applies to
all houses; skirmish and multiplayer retain a separate serialized choice for
each house slot.

The Dune2 skin includes the currently authored per-house zone and special-
building Compacts, matching construction and properties-panel portraits,
residential growth stages, transparent cell replacement, and an alpha-aware
Scale2x/Scale3x path isolated from the classic indexed-palette scaler.

Android additionally packages the complete current skin payload and preserves
existing saves and configuration during upgrade.
