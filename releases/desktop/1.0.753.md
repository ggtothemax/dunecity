# Dune City 1.0.753

Campaigns start directly with installer-matched mods eligible for hot join. Custom mods use the pregame lobby, and online games no longer require an empty player slot: players can spectate or take over an AI.

- Compressed snapshots reduce joining transfers while preserving deterministic state.
- Hosts can change shared game settings during play; all players have a pause control, and leaving the host menu releases its automatic pause.
- QuantBot balance includes campaign technology restrictions, revised economy and attack behavior, palace limits, and custom-game city limits by map size and difficulty. Brutal cities remain uncapped.
- Added construction-yard limits, a confirmed Skip mission action, and improved results/mod selection screens.
- Fixed reported Starport/sandworm crashes and related multiplayer controls.
- Dune City mod is independently versioned1.002; the app is1.0.753. Nuclear power still unlocks at campaign level6.

All multiplayer participants need this release's compatible network protocol. Older saves remain supported. Updates preserve the separate saves, settings and user-mod profile.

macOS, Windows installer and supported AppImage installations can use the in-game updater. DEB/RPM and portable installations use their normal manual update procedure.
