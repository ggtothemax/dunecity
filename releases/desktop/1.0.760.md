# Dune City 1.0.760

Carryalls, ornithopters and ground units now follow Dune Dynasty movement and turning rates across DuneCity, Vanilla and Dune2R, including carryall approach/docking and ground tile transitions. Built-in unit INI templates are updated alongside the engine changes.

Rockets now use Dynasty flight, steering, arming and explosion behavior. Launcher anti-air fire retains its distinct targeting behavior, and turret rockets can physically intercept aircraft so ornithopters remain vulnerable without remote-hit or full-splash bonuses.

City AI now prioritizes crime protection over optional growth. Human-shared AI helpers respect their difficulty population ceiling while human construction and natural population growth remain unrestricted.

Multiplayer protocol 18: all participants should update together. This release retains save format 9845.

Validated with 16 passing native regression targets, real-engine projectile combat and save-continuation scenarios, a city-AI spending simulation, and packaged macOS runtime checks.
